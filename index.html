the story behind the name

Falafel is my comfort food. It's crispy on the outside, soft on the inside, and somehow holds everything together even when it looks like it might fall apart. Honestly? Same.

I'm an IGCSE student currently in the middle of what I can only describe as a slow-motion burnout. School is a lot. Nobody around me really gets the things I care about — the games, the lore, the 3am character analysis spirals. So I did what any reasonable person would do: I started talking to strangers on the internet instead.

This blog is my outlet. A place to put the feelings that don't fit anywhere else — too weird for school, too much for a group chat, just right for here.
